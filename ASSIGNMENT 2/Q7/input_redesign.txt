Input Workload Configurations for Q7 Day-4 Design Challenge:

Workload 1: Starvation Stress Test
- Initial arrival: 1 Severity-5 Patient (Asha) at timestamp 0.
- Subsequent arrivals: 2,000 continuous Severity 1 and Severity 2 emergency arrivals.
- Goal: Measure whether Asha ever gets treated, or suffers infinite starvation.

Workload 2: Standard Scaled Hospital Volume (5,000 Patients)
- Represents daily patient volume of a metropolitan hospital.
- Evaluates admission latency, consultation service latency, and memory footprint.

Workload 3: Regional Trauma Network Volume (20,000 Patients)
- Evaluates multi-hour peak surge conditions across five clinical urgency tiers.

Workload 4: Pandemic / Mass Casualty Disaster Rush (50,000 Patients)
- Extreme stress test exposing the asymptotic difference between O(log N) binary heap and O(1) multi-level circular deques.
